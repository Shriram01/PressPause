# 🎮 PressPause

> **Pause. Plan. Play.**

PressPause is a modern gaming companion built to help players organize their game library, manage their backlog, discover what to play next, and gain meaningful insights into their gaming habits.

Unlike traditional game trackers, PressPause aims to become a complete gaming operating system that combines library management, planning, recommendations, analytics, and community features into a single platform.

---

## ✨ Vision

Today's gamers often own hundreds of games across multiple platforms, making it difficult to decide what to play next or keep track of their progress.

PressPause aims to solve this by becoming the central hub for every gamer.
